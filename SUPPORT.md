# Getting help

- **Questions about using a project**: open an issue in that project's
  repository and choose the most suitable template.
- **Found a bug or want a change**: open an issue using the *Bug report* or
  *Change request* template.
- **Security problem**: do not open an issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Digital Office repositories are usually maintained by one or two people
alongside other work. We will respond as soon as we can, but cannot guarantee
response times for general questions.
