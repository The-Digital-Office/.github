## The Digital Office for Scottish Local Government

We work with Scottish councils to design, build and share digital services,
standards and tools.

Our public repositories are open for reuse by other public bodies and anyone
else who finds them useful.

- **Code** is published under the MIT licence.
- **Documentation** is published under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
- **Software** repositories describe themselves with
  [publiccode.yml](https://yml.publiccode.tools), so they can be found and
  catalogued alongside public-sector software from other countries.

Found a security problem? Please follow our
[security policy](https://github.com/The-Digital-Office/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
rather than opening an issue.
